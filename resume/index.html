Case Study 01

Wallets Africa

Co-Founder & VP Product — Jan 2017 to Sep 2019 · Lagos, NG

Cross-border banking Jan 2017 – Sep 2019 Lagos, Nigeria
Payments Cards Cross-border Risk & Fraud PCI-DSS 0 → 1

Context

Cross-border financial infrastructure in West Africa was fragmented, expensive, and inaccessible to most users. There was no unified platform that combined wallets, cards, and remittance into a single product experience built for the local market. I co-founded Wallets Africa to close that gap — building from zero with no existing product, no team, and no playbook.

$3B+
Weekly transaction volume at scale
50K+
Users acquired within the first year
70%
Reduction in fraud losses via ML risk scoring

Approach

I owned every dimension of product: strategy, delivery, operations, and risk. The first architectural decision was to build for composability — wallets, cards, and cross-border rails as modular layers rather than a monolith — so we could extend quickly without rewrites. This bet paid off when we integrated Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, and Coinbase, each adding a meaningful payment rail without touching core infrastructure.

The fraud problem was existential early on. Rather than rule-based blocking (which damages conversion), I directed the design of an ML-based risk scoring system layered with real-time transaction monitoring and a dispute management workflow. The result was a 70% reduction in fraud losses without a corresponding drop in legitimate transaction approval rates — a threshold that satisfied regulators and kept unit economics healthy.

I also led the PCI-DSS certification process end-to-end, treating compliance not as a legal checkbox but as a product foundation that unlocked enterprise partnerships and gave users a reason to trust us with increasing transaction volumes.

"Built the product organisation from the ground up — the culture, delivery frameworks, hiring roadmap, and cross-functional governance that underpinned $3B+ in weekly volume."

Outcomes

Wallets Africa reached 50,000+ users within year one and scaled to $3B+ in weekly transaction volume. Crypto-linked wallets and cross-border remittance products unlocked new revenue streams post-Coinbase integration. The risk infrastructure reduced fraud losses by 70% while maintaining healthy conversion rates. PCI-DSS certification positioned the company for regulated enterprise partnerships that would have been otherwise unreachable at that stage.

Yassir is a 13M+ user super app operating across Africa, Europe, and the Middle East. When I joined as the senior product voice for financial services, the wallet and core banking systems were architected for a single market — making each new country expansion a months-long engineering project. The financial services product had headroom to become a primary platform, not a secondary feature, but only if the infrastructure could move as fast as the commercial opportunity demanded.

Approach

The first move was an architectural overhaul of the core banking and wallet stack. This wasn't a technical decision delegated to engineering — I drove the product foundations: what abstractions the system needed, where the boundaries between markets should sit, and how open-loop integrations could be handled without bespoke per-market builds. That work compressed new market launch time from four months to under one week.

With the infrastructure unlocked, I led wallet expansion across France, Tunisia, South Africa, Algeria, Morocco, and Senegal — coordinating regulatory approvals, localisation strategy, and go-to-market execution simultaneously across three continents with distributed teams. Case Study 03

Boyd Gaming

Gaming Application Product Lead — Dec 2025 to Present · Toronto, ON

Regulated iGaming Dec 2025 – Present Ontario · Alberta · Pennsylvania · Nevada
iGaming KYC & Onboarding Biometrics / MFA Apple Pay · Google Pay CMS Multi-jurisdiction

Context

Boyd Gaming operates regulated gaming apps across Ontario, Alberta, Pennsylvania, and Nevada — each jurisdiction with its own compliance framework, identity verification requirements, and player protection obligations. The Stardust app's onboarding funnel was losing players at identity verification, and the deposit experience was lagging behind consumer expectations for mobile payment UX. My remit: own product strategy and roadmap across all jurisdictions, and move the key conversion metrics without compromising regulatory standing.

20%
Drop-off reduction in identity verification flows
4+
Regulated jurisdictions managed concurrently
10+
Competitive products benchmarked in UX audit

Approach

I started with a structured UX audit of the Stardust onboarding experience, benchmarking against 10+ competitive products to locate exactly where the registration-to-deposit funnel was losing players and why. The insight was that the identity verification step was designed around regulatory compliance rather than user intent — it asked for everything upfront, in a sequence that didn't map to how players approached the product.

The solution was a progressive KYC framework using biometrics and MFA: collect only what's required at each stage of the player journey, then layer in additional verification as trust and activity levels increase. This reduced drop-off in identity verification flows by 20% while satisfying the compliance requirements of all four jurisdictions simultaneously — a meaningful constraint because what passes in Ontario doesn't automatically satisfy Pennsylvania or Alberta.

In parallel, I drove the delivery of Apple Pay and Google Pay integrations to close the gap between deposit intent and deposit completion for mobile-first players. I also built out merchandising capabilities and CMS tooling so that marketing teams could operate independently of engineering — removing a persistent bottleneck in promotional campaign execution and improving time-to-market for offers.

"Designed a progressive KYC framework that reduced identity verification drop-off by 20% while satisfying compliance requirements across four regulated jurisdictions simultaneously."

Outcomes

The progressive KYC framework cut identity verification drop-off by 20%, directly improving the registration-to-deposit conversion rate. Apple Pay and Google Pay integrations improved deposit completion for mobile-first users and reduced checkout friction. The CMS and merchandising layer gave marketing teams full autonomy over promotional execution, removing engineering as a dependency for time-sensitive campaigns. The UX audit and funnel redesign established a benchmark framework now used across the broader multi-jurisdiction product estate.

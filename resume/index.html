Built a cross-border banking platform from zero to $3B+ weekly volume.

No existing product, no team, no playbook — and a Y Combinator backing along the way. Composable wallets, ML risk scoring, and PCI-DSS as a product foundation, not a checkbox.

Context

Cross-border financial infrastructure in West Africa was fragmented, expensive, and inaccessible. There was no unified platform combining wallets, cards, and remittance built for the local market. I co-founded Wallets Africa to close that gap — building from zero with no existing product, no team, and no playbook. The company went on to be backed by Y Combinator.

Approach

Owned every dimension of product: strategy, delivery, operations, and risk. The first architectural decision was composability — wallets, cards, and cross-border rails as modular layers — so we could extend without rewrites. The bet paid off when we integrated Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, and Coinbase. Each added a meaningful payment rail without touching core infrastructure.

Outcomes

50,000+ users in year one, scaling to $3B+ in weekly transaction volume. Crypto-linked wallets and cross-border remittance unlocked new revenue streams. Risk infrastructure cut fraud losses 70%. PCI-DSS opened regulated enterprise partnerships otherwise unreachable at that stage.

"Built the product organisation from the ground up — the culture, delivery frameworks, hiring roadmap, and cross-functional governance that underpinned $3B+ in weekly volume."

Compressed multi-market launch from four months to under a week.

Architectural overhaul of core banking for a 13M+ user super app. France, Tunisia, South Africa, Algeria, Morocco, Senegal — a single platform, six rollouts.

Context

Yassir is a 13M+ user super app across Africa, Europe, and the Middle East. When I joined, wallet and core-banking systems were architected for a single market — making each new country a months-long engineering project. Financial services had headroom to become a primary platform, but only if infrastructure could move at the speed of commercial opportunity.

Approach

I drove the product foundations of a core-banking and wallet rebuild: what abstractions the system needed, where market boundaries should sit, and how open-loop integrations could be handled without bespoke per-market builds. With infrastructure unlocked, I led wallet expansion across France, Tunisia, South Africa, Algeria, Morocco, and Senegal — coordinating regulatory approvals, localisation, and GTM simultaneously.

Outcomes

Wallet adoption reached 60%+ of active users with sustained ~10% MoM growth. Agent Wallet crossed 10M monthly transactions. Market-launch dropped from four months to under one week. Payment orchestration became the foundation for multi-market PSP strategy.

"Sustained ~10% month-over-month wallet growth through product-led engagement, lifecycle optimisation, and 60%+ adoption of the active user base."

Designed progressive KYC that cut verification drop-off by 20%.

Four regulators, one funnel. Re-sequencing identity verification around player intent — without weakening any jurisdiction's controls.

Context

Boyd Gaming operates regulated apps across Ontario, Alberta, Pennsylvania, and Nevada — each jurisdiction with its own compliance framework, identity requirements, and player-protection obligations. Stardust was losing players at identity verification, and the deposit experience lagged consumer expectations for mobile payment UX.

Approach

Started with a structured UX audit benchmarked against 10+ competitive products. The insight: identity verification was designed around regulatory compliance rather than user intent. The fix was a progressive KYC framework — collect only what's required at each stage, layer additional verification as trust and activity rise. In parallel I drove Apple Pay and Google Pay integrations and built CMS tooling that decoupled marketing from engineering.

Outcomes

Progressive KYC cut verification drop-off by 20%. Apple Pay and Google Pay reduced checkout friction. The CMS layer gave marketing full autonomy over promotional execution. The funnel-redesign methodology now serves as a benchmark across the broader multi-jurisdiction estate.

"Designed a progressive KYC framework that reduced identity verification drop-off by 20% while satisfying compliance across four regulated jurisdictions simultaneously."
